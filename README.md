### 📌 About Me
Product Owner and Systems Developer with a track record of architecting scalable enterprise tools, governing digital infrastructure across nationwide networks, and designing workflows that cut down cognitive load. 

I bridge the gap between heavy enterprise backend logic and human-centered interfaces, operating on a simple principle: **software should eliminate manual friction, not create more meetings.**
